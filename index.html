Vision

Become a leading Malaysian investment and trading firm, evolving from a founder-led venture into a lasting public institution.

Mission

Grow capital through disciplined trading and investment, while strengthening Malaysia's financial markets.

Five objectives.

  1. 01Build capital by trading and investing, and reinvest profits.
  2. 02Build professional skills in trading, research, risk management, financial analysis and operations.
  3. 03Prioritise Malaysian financial markets.
  4. 04Create long-term shareholder value through responsible allocation and governance.
  5. 05Build an institution that can outlast its founders, possibly becoming a public company.
